Welcome to RockLab

A free tech demo from Studio Gray Lake. Build rock up one step a tab: one stone, a cluster of stones, a cliff of clusters, and a land of hills with rock laid over it.

Drag to turn, scroll to zoom, right-drag to move. Point at a slider to see what it does. Try "how much rock shows" on the Cliff tab first.

RockLab is 3D and works best on a computer. On a touch screen, pinch to zoom.
